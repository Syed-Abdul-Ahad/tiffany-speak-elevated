Title

Founder

Tiffany N. Haywood is the founder of Transform & Thrive Holistics, LLC, the foundation of the work she carries today.

Her journey into this work did not begin as a business. It began as a personal need for change.

In a season where she felt misaligned, overwhelmed, and disconnected from herself, Tiffany began doing the internal work to reset her life, starting with her health and daily habits, and expanding into her mindset, emotional well-being, and environment.

What started as a focus on wellness and feeling better physically quickly became something deeper. She realized that true transformation required more than surface-level change. It required addressing the patterns, thoughts, and behaviors that were keeping her stuck.

As she committed to that process, she experienced a shift in clarity, discipline, and personal alignment that changed how she lived, showed up, and made decisions.

Transform & Thrive Holistics was created as a reflection of that journey, a space where she could begin sharing what she was learning and living in real time.

Over time, that personal work evolved into a structured approach now known as Detox Your Life, a methodology that helps individuals identify what is out of alignment and intentionally rebuild from within.

Today, that foundation continues to shape everything she does across speaking, mentorship, and holistic detox work.

Because this work is not just something she teaches. It is something she has lived and continues to live.

Title

Transformational Speaker

Building on the foundation of her work, Tiffany is a transformational speaker on identity, leadership, and personal alignment, known for delivering messages that move audiences beyond motivation into measurable internal shift.

She speaks to organizations, institutions, and audiences who are ready to move out of autopilot and into intentional living and leadership.

Her talks challenge individuals to take ownership of their patterns, confront uncomfortable truths, and recalibrate how they think, lead, and show up.

In corporate environments, her message supports leaders in shifting from pressure-driven performance to grounded, self-led authority.

In academic and personal development spaces, she equips individuals with the awareness and tools needed to build identity before burnout defines them.

Every keynote delivers practical frameworks, reflection tools, and a clear pathway for transformation that extends beyond the stage.

Title

Mentor

While many are introduced to Tiffany through her speaking, her work extends beyond the stage into deeper, more personal transformation through mentorship and guided experiences.

Her mentorship is rooted in clarity, self-discovery, and alignment, helping individuals break patterns, rebuild identity, and move forward with intention.

She creates spaces where people can be honest about where they are, release what they have been carrying, and develop the discipline to choose differently.

Through structured conversations, reflective practices, and strategic guidance, Tiffany supports her clients in:

  • Reclaiming their voice and personal authority
  • Strengthening decision making and boundaries
  • Shifting habits that no longer align with who they are becoming
  • Building a life that feels clear, grounded, and sustainable

Her approach is not about dependency. It is about equipping individuals to trust themselves and lead their own transformation.

Title

Holistic Detox Specialist

At the core of Tiffany's work is her framework methodology, Detox Your Life, a holistic approach to transformation that addresses the full spectrum of mind, body, and spirit.

Rooted in her own journey, this work goes beyond traditional ideas of detox and expands into how individuals think, live, and exist daily.

She teaches that detoxing is not just about what you remove physically, but what you release mentally, emotionally, and environmentally.

Her work focuses on identifying and clearing toxic influences such as:

  • Limiting beliefs and mental clutter
  • Unhealthy habits and behavioral patterns
  • Misaligned environments and relationships
  • Emotional weight that has gone unprocessed

Through this process, individuals learn how to create space for clarity, peace, and intentional living.

This work is expressed through her speaking, mentorship, digital resources, and evolving wellness-based tools, including guided detox experiences and supportive lifestyle practices.

All designed to support lasting transformation from within.
