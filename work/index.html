THE METHOD

The Detox Your Life Framework

Transformation begins with awareness. The Detox Your Life framework addresses three interconnected dimensions that shape how you think, act, and live.

Mind

Examine the beliefs, internal narratives, and identity attachments shaping your decisions.

Body

Assess daily habits, standards, boundaries, and behavioral patterns that either support or sabotage your growth.

Spirit

Clarify your alignment with purpose, values, and integrity. This is where intention becomes responsibility.

Every experience below is built around this framework.

YOUR STARTING POINT

Start With Awareness

Before joining a program or entering a community, clarity matters. The Detox Your Life Assessment is a structured self-evaluation designed to identify where misalignment may be present across your Mind, Body, and Spirit.

In less than 10 minutes, you will receive personalized results outlining:

  • Your dominant detox area
  • Patterns that may be sustaining stagnation
  • A recommended next step for realignment
